## Airline-by-airline strategy

### LEVEL (Iberia's long-haul low-cost) ★★★★

LEVEL operates Barcelona-USA with **lie-flat seats in "LEVEL Premium" cabin** (12 seats per plane). Routes:
- BCN → JFK, BOS, EWR, MIA, LAX, SFO.

LEVEL Premium prices:
- Sweet spot Feb/Sept: **€900-1.250 RT** lie-flat.
- High season: €1.400-1.800 RT.

**Trampa**: LEVEL Premium is **NOT** full business class — smaller cabin, fewer amenities. But seat goes flat, which is the most important.

### Norse Atlantic ★★★

Long-haul low-cost from London, Paris, Berlin to USA. Norse Premium has premium economy that **does not go full-flat** but extra legroom + decent service.

For Madrid origin: book **MAD → CDG/LGW + Norse Atlantic to USA**. Total RT: €900-1.200.

### TAP Portugal via Lisbon ★★★★

TAP operates Madrid → Lisbon → New York/Miami/Boston with **A330neo lie-flat business class**. Sweet spot pricing:
- Feb/Sept: **€1.200-1.500 RT business**.
- Includes free TAP Stopover Lisbon 1-7 nights.

**Veredicto**: TAP business class is **the best value** for Madrid → USA business under €1.500 consistently.

### Iberia Business "Light" fare ★★★

Iberia has **Business Light** = full business class with 1 piece checked + meal + lie-flat, but **no lounge, no Avios accrual, no flex changes**. Sweet spot:
- Feb/Sept: **€1.400-1.800 RT**.

If you don't need lounges or Avios, **Business Light is real business at lower price**.

### Error fares Iberia/AA ★★★★★ (when they happen)

Iberia error fares to USA happen **2-3 times per year**:
- Sept 2023: MAD-MIA business €560 RT error fare (lasted 3h).
- Mar 2024: MAD-JFK business €890 RT error fare (lasted 6h).
- Nov 2024: MAD-LAX business €1.100 RT error fare (lasted 18h).

These require **alert subscription + immediate booking** (sub 1h response). Subscribe to [our alerts](/alertas).

### American Airlines via JFK/MIA ★★★

AA Business Class Spain to USA. Sweet spot prices:
- Feb/Sept: €1.500-2.000 RT.
- High season: €2.500-3.500 RT.

Mid-tier option. Better with British Airways code-share to LHR + onward.

### British Airways Club Suite ★★★★

### Lufthansa via FRA ★★★★

LH Allegris business class is launching 2025-2026 — **suites with door**. Pricing:
- Sweet spot Feb/Sept: €1.600-2.100 RT.
- Awards Miles & More 105.000 miles + tax.

### Air France via CDG ★★★★

Air France A350 business class (excellent product). Pricing:
- Sweet spot: €1.500-2.000 RT.
- Awards Flying Blue: 80.000-130.000 miles.

## Miles strategy: 85k Avios = €1.500 effective

Iberia/BA Avios award MAD-JFK:
- **62.500 Avios + €380 tax** one-way Iberia business (off-peak).
- **85.000 Avios + €380 tax** one-way Iberia business (peak).

If you can accumulate 170.000 Avios (off-peak RT), business class costs **€760 cash + 170k Avios**. Avios are earned via:

- **Iberia credit card sign-up bonus**: 30k-50k Avios.
- **AmEx Platinum bonus** (transferable 1:1 to Avios): 30k-45k.
- **Iberia Plus partner shopping**: 5k-15k per quarter.
- **Iberia/BA flying**: variable.

Realistic 12-month accumulation: **120-180k Avios** without flying much.

## FAQ business class to USA from Spain

**Is LEVEL Premium real business class?**

No — it's premium economy with lie-flat seat. Lounges and amenities less than full business. But functionally similar for sleeping.

**Why is Iberia direct cheaper than via Lisbon TAP?**

It's not always. **Compare both** — sometimes TAP via LIS is €200-400 cheaper for business than Iberia direct.

**How much Avios needed for free MAD-USA business?**

62.500 off-peak / 85.000 peak Avios + €380 tax for ONE WAY Iberia business. RT = double.

**Error fares are real?**

Yes — they happen 2-3x year Spain-USA business. Lasted 3-18 hours each time. Requires alert subscription.

**Best month overall for cheap business?**

**February and late September**. Combine sweet spot pricing with alert subscription = high success rate.

**Can I do Madrid → Hawaii business under €1.500?**

Rarely. Hawaii adds 2 stopovers + premium pricing. Realistic: €1.800-2.500 RT business.

**Is Norse Atlantic Premium "good"?**

Acceptable for budget hunters. Premium economy with extra recline + meal. **Does NOT lie flat**.
