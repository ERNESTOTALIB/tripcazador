A free stopover program lets you visit a 2nd city on your way to the destination at no extra airfare cost — sometimes with free hotel nights included. We compared the 8 major airline stopover programs in 2026 to find the real winners (and the ones that are PR but not value).

## TL;DR rankings

1. **Turkish Airlines TourIstanbul** — 2 free hotel nights in Istanbul (best deal)
2. **Qatar Airways Stopover** — 1 night $14, up to 4 nights with discount
3. **Icelandair Stopover** — Up to 7 days free in Iceland (no hotel)
4. **Singapore Airlines Stopover** — 1 night ~$100 hotel discount via SG Stopover
5. **Etihad Stopover Abu Dhabi** — 1-2 nights with discounted hotel
6. **TAP Portugal Stopover** — Up to 5 days free in Lisbon/Porto (no hotel)
7. **KLM Stopover Amsterdam** — Same airfare, no extras (just a feature, not a "program")
8. **Emirates Dubai Stopover** — Discounted hotel only, not free

## Detailed comparison

### Turkish Airlines TourIstanbul (winner for value)

- **Eligibility**: round-trip booking with Turkish, with stopover in Istanbul of 20-72h between connections.
- **What you get**: 2 free nights at 4-5* Istanbul hotel + half-day city tour ($25 for tour). Hotel is real, not "value at $50". Tested at Hilton Bomonti, Conrad Bosphorus, etc.
- **Eligible passengers**: Economy class on long-haul connecting routes (e.g. MAD-IST-NRT, BCN-IST-BKK).
- **Real value**: $300-450 saved (2 hotel nights) per traveler. Best deal on the list.
- **Catch**: must book directly via Turkish; some agencies don't offer it.

### Qatar Airways Stopover (best for short stops)

- **Eligibility**: passengers on Qatar with ≥4h connection in Doha.
- **What you get**: 1 night premium hotel for $14 (yes, $14!), up to 4 nights with discount on additional. Hotels include W Doha, Crowne Plaza Doha.
- **Real value**: $200-350 saved per night (Doha hotels are expensive).
- **Catch**: qualifying connection must be 8-23h for hotel benefit. Some routes only offer breakfast.

### Icelandair Stopover (best for extending)

- **Eligibility**: passengers on Icelandair flying transatlantic (EU↔USA via KEF).
- **What you get**: up to 7 days free in Iceland. NO free hotel; must arrange your own.
- **Real value**: zero airfare added vs same itinerary. Iceland is the "destination" essentially. Hotel ~€130-180/night though, so budget needed.
- **Catch**: more expensive than other airline routings to USA usually. Net value depends on Iceland appetite.

### Singapore Airlines Stopover (best for SE Asia)

- **Eligibility**: passengers on Singapore Airlines on Star Alliance long-haul, ≥6h connection in SIN.
- **What you get**: SG Stopover Holiday — 1 night at 3-5* hotel, $100-180 booking fee added to ticket but ~$300+ hotel value, includes attractions tickets ($30 included).
- **Real value**: $150-250 net positive per night booking.
- **Catch**: only on specific itineraries and class booking.

### Etihad Stopover Abu Dhabi

- **Eligibility**: Etihad passengers with ≥10h connection in AUH.
- **What you get**: 1-2 nights at participating hotel ($30-60 booking fee).
- **Real value**: $200-300 net positive per night.
- **Catch**: limited hotel selection, must book ≥7 days ahead.

### TAP Portugal Stopover (best for Lisbon/Porto)

- **Eligibility**: TAP passengers transiting through LIS or OPO en route Europe-Americas.
- **What you get**: up to 5 days in Lisbon or Porto with no extra airfare. NO free hotel.
- **Real value**: zero airfare difference + Lisbon/Porto are great cities to "trip-hack". Hotels €70-130/night additional cost.
- **Catch**: only on TAP-operated transatlantic routes.

### KLM Stopover Amsterdam (basic feature)

- **Eligibility**: KLM transatlantic passengers via AMS.
- **What you get**: layover up to 24h, no special hotel.
- **Real value**: standard transit, not a "program". Listed for completeness.

### Emirates Dubai Stopover (least valuable)

- **Eligibility**: Emirates transatlantic passengers via DXB.
- **What you get**: discounted hotel rate, ~$130-200/night (vs $200-350 normal).
- **Real value**: ~$100/night net positive.
- **Catch**: must book through Emirates Holidays add-on. Real Dubai costs are still high.

## Real-world examples (mistake fares + stopover combos)

### Madrid → Tokyo via Doha with stopover

- Mistake fare Qatar MAD-NRT (€480 economy) + Stopover 1 night Doha ($14) = visit Doha for $14 hotel + airfare unchanged.
- Total trip cost: €480 + $14 = ~€500.

### Barcelona → Bangkok via Istanbul with TourIstanbul

- Mistake fare Turkish BCN-BKK (€420 economy) + 2 free nights Istanbul hotel.
- Total trip cost: €420 + $25 city tour = ~€440.
- Effectively: visited Istanbul for free as part of the trip.

### Madrid → Bali via Singapore

- Mistake fare Singapore Airlines MAD-DPS via SIN (€620 economy) + SG Stopover 2 nights at Marina Bay Sands area = $250 booking fee but $500 hotel value.
- Effective extra cost for 2 nights Singapore: $250 vs $500 normal hotel = save $250.

## Tips for booking stopover combinations

1. **Mistake fares first**: identify the error fare, then check the airline's stopover program.
2. **Book direct**: most stopover deals require direct airline booking. OTAs (Skyscanner, Kayak) don't always allow them.
3. **Check connection length**: programs require minimum hours (4h-10h). Don't book the cheapest tickets if they have shorter connections.
4. **Visa requirements**: Doha 96h visa-free for many EU. Istanbul transit visa-free 24h, otherwise e-visa needed for stopover.
5. **Combine with hub-bridging**: some stopover programs let you switch to a competing airline mid-trip if needed (rare, check terms).

## Conclusion

For real value: **Turkish TourIstanbul + Qatar Stopover** are the two clear winners for free/cheap accommodation. **Icelandair + TAP** are the best for "free extension" of itineraries. **Emirates** is overrated in 2026.

If your itinerary takes you through DOH or IST hubs naturally, always check if a stopover deal applies. Could save €300-500 per traveler.
