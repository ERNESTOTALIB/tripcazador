Most travel sites tell you to fly to Bali in **April-May or September** — they're right that those are good months. But they miss something better: **November**. Here's why November is the cheapest practical month from Europe to Denpasar (DPS), and how to capture sub-€450 fares when they appear.

## The November paradox

Bali has two monsoons. The "wet" one runs roughly **November to March**. The "dry" one is **April to October**. Conventional wisdom: avoid the wet months because the weather sucks.

What this misses:

1. **November rain in Bali ≠ all-day rain**. Real pattern is short heavy showers (1-2 hours afternoon) followed by sun. Locals call it *musim hujan ringan* — light rainy season. Beach mornings, beach afternoons, dinner indoors.
2. **Flight prices crater in early November** because:
   - Australian school holidays end (their winter break is over).
   - European long-haul demand to Asia drops between summer/Christmas peaks.
   - Garuda Indonesia, Qatar, Turkish, Singapore Airlines all run promo cycles.
3. **Bali in November is 30% cheaper on accommodation** vs July-August. Same hotels.

Net result: you get 80% of the dry-season experience at 60% of the cost.

## When NOT to go to Bali

- **Late December → early January (Christmas/Indonesian New Year)**: peak prices, peak crowds, peak everything.
- **Australian school holidays late June and early October**: Bali floods with Australian families. Hotels 2x normal.
- **Nyepi Day (Day of Silence, 31 March 2026)**: airport closed for 24 hours, no transport. Plan around it.
- **Galungan/Kuningan (variable, ~10 days every 6 months)**: religious festival. Beautiful but tourist services often closed.
