If you're flying with Turkish Airlines from Madrid or Barcelona to Asia, Africa, or further Eastern Europe, there's a program 80% of passengers don't know exists: **Turkish Airlines Stopover**, offering 1-2 nights free in a 4-5★ Istanbul hotel during your layover. This is one of the best free hospitality programs in commercial aviation 2026.

## What's included

The Turkish Airlines free stopover program (officially "Touristanbul" + "Stopover Istanbul") provides at no extra cost:

**Tier 1 — Touristanbul (escalas 6-23h)**:
- **Free guided city tour** (4-6h, en bus, English/Spanish/French groups available).
- **Lunch included** at restaurant near tour route.
- **Skip-the-line entry** to Hagia Sophia Mosque + Sultanahmet area.
- Departure point: Istanbul Airport (IST), exit pickup terminal arrivals.

**Tier 2 — Stopover Istanbul (escalas 24h+ to 7 days)**:
- **1 night free 4★ hotel** for Economy class passengers.
- **2 nights free 5★ hotel** for Business class passengers.
- **Hotel transfer** airport-hotel-airport included.
- **Breakfast included** at hotel restaurant.

The accommodation tier varies based on availability — usually Hilton Istanbul Bomonti, Mövenpick Istanbul Asia, or similar 4-5★ in central Sultanahmet/Beyoglu/Fatih.

## Eligibility requirements 2026

Verified May 2026:

1. **Round-trip ticket** Turkish Airlines (TK code) — codeshare ticketed by partner won't qualify.
2. **Connecting through IST** Istanbul Airport — flight starts and ends outside Turkey.
3. **Layover duration**:
   - 6-23h → Touristanbul tour eligible.
   - 24h+ to 7 days → Free hotel eligible.
4. **Cabin class**:
   - Economy → 1 night 4★ hotel.
   - Business → 2 nights 5★ hotel.
5. **Visa**: Spanish/EU citizens get e-Visa or visa-on-arrival within 90 days, no problem.
6. **Booking timing**: stopover must be requested at booking OR within 72h of departure via "Manage Booking" on turkishairlines.com.
7. **NOT eligible**: pure round-trip Spain → Istanbul → Spain (where Istanbul is the destination, not a stopover).

## How to claim it (step-by-step)

**Method A — At booking (recommended)**:
1. Book on turkishairlines.com (NOT through Skyscanner/Kayak — those route through OTAs that don't propagate stopover request).
2. In the booking flow, after selecting flights, you'll see "Add Stopover Service" option (€0).
3. Select hotel preference and stopover length (1 night Economy, 2 nights Business).
4. Confirmation email arrives within 24h with hotel voucher and transfer instructions.

**Method B — After booking**:
1. Login to turkishairlines.com → Manage Booking.
2. Click "Stopover Istanbul" service.
3. Select hotel from available list.
4. Submit (subject to availability — book 1-2 weeks before departure for best hotel selection).

**Method C — Phone (last resort)**:
- Turkish Airlines call center +34 91 122 49 70 (Spain), 24/7.
- Reference your booking PNR.
- Agents have authority to add stopover even with last-minute requests.

## Common mistakes to avoid

**1. Booking through Skyscanner/Kayak/OTAs**: many OTAs strip the stopover service from booking — you end up with a "regular" ticket without the perk. Book direct via turkishairlines.com.

**2. Not requesting stopover within 72h**: if you forget, the request is harder post-departure (sometimes denied). Set a reminder for the moment you book the flight.

**3. Layover under 6h**: if connection is 4-5h, you don't qualify for Touristanbul tour AND it's risky to go into city (need 3.5-4h minimum to do anything meaningful + buffer for return).

**4. Overpacking the day**: trying to see Hagia Sophia + Topkapı + Blue Mosque + Bazaar + Bosphorus cruise in 24h → exhausted, jet-lagged, miss flight. Pick 3 max activities.

**5. Forgetting Istanbulkart**: pay-as-you-go transit card (€1 deposit + balance) is universal. Without it, you're stuck taking taxis (€15-25 each way to Sultanahmet from many hotels — adds up fast).

**6. Buying breakfast outside hotel**: included one is good (Turkish breakfast spread). Save the €10-15.

## When stopover doesn't make sense

Be honest with yourself:

- **Layover <6h**: skip the tour, just chill in the airport (Pegasus Lounge €35 day pass is huge).
- **You hate cities/crowds**: Istanbul is INTENSE — 16M people, traffic, noise. If you prefer rural, skip stopover.
- **Bad weather forecast**: November-March it can be cold rain. Check before booking — alternatively use the night for sleep at hotel and skip outdoor activities.
- **Ramadan period (March 20-April 19, 2026)**: city operates differently, restaurants/cafés may be closed during day. Logistics harder.
- **Coming from a long-haul flight 12h+**: jet lag means you'll waste the day half-zombie. Stopover better for outbound (Madrid → Asia direction) than return (Asia → Madrid where you arrive exhausted).
